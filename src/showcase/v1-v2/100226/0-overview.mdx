## 1 · Player rows v2

What Weston asked for:

- Every player open by default, with no expand-all or collapse-all, because *"it adds an extra step"*.
- **Add** on every row, without expanding it. *"Walking or riding? 9 or 18? You're paying for
  yourself? Yes."* Then Add, Add, Pay. *"This is going to be the most used thing in all of our apps…
  it just has to be fast."*
- The tee fee and the transport fee stay on the row, and names are shown in full.
- Expanding is only for changing something: Change golfer, No-show, a different rate.
- Touch-sized controls.

Justin decided that **Add puts the player straight onto the register's order**, and the rail opens.
The mock has two lines per player, and all four of King, D.'s foursome fit without scrolling.

## 8 · League view v2

What Weston asked for: speed, and checking in golfers who arrive at the same time, e.g. *"Justin's
here, pay. Johnny, check in, pay."* Justin chose to show all four of these:

- **A separate League view.** The normal per-tee-time flow stays the default, and a league's tee
  time links to the League view.
- **Placing the roster on tee times**, ahead of time or on the day: *"Justin, you're the 7:20."*
- **Checking in without leaving the screen**, with search, Check in and Pay on every golfer, and a
  quick trip to the register for an extra.
- **Shotgun or tee times**, plus **several leagues in one day**.
