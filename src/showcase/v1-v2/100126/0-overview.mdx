# 100126 · Feedback round from the Sep 30 call with Weston

Proposals from Justin's call with Weston Farnsworth on Sep 30: the reservation panel, the order
rail, leagues, the main menu, Settings and the phone. Each change has its own entry in this folder,
with two stories:

- **Original**: the live V1 → V2 screen as it is today. The phone items use Weston Edits' phone,
  because V1 → V2 is tablet-only.
- **Proposal**: a mockup of the change, built in Storybook only and marked **PROPOSAL · 100126**
  in its corner. Nothing in the app changes until a proposal is approved.

Weston's own priorities on the call: fit the foursome, put membership back, add the order number to
Financial, then *"let's make sure everything else is in order"*. He is *"not as worried about events
… to start"*, and happy with the tee sheet and the slide-over *"for now"*.

> **Since the Oct 2 review (100226):** Weston approved items 2–7 and 9–12, and they are now built
> into the prototype. Because each **Original** story renders the live app, the Originals for those
> items now show the new design; the **Proposal** mocks still show what was proposed. Items 1 and 8
> went another round, see **V1 → V2 Migration › 100226**.

## Not in this round

- **A Starter or Ranger view.** A separate, phone-first view to check in, see paid, and assign
  carts and keys, with no money on it. Weston likes the idea and suggested *"maybe it's not even
  tied into Birdie"*. Parked.
- **Tips, how-tos or release notes in the empty rail.** Parked so the scope stays small.
- **A redesigned customer record**, with View ID and ID.me: a round of its own.
- **The Buck settings concepts** and reviewing Buck's pages before they're retired: Buck work, not
  this prototype.
