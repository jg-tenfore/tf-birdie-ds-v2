(2:46, September 2026).

Both are the **18-hole club** with the edits switched on. The other six prototypes are
unchanged, except for the bug fixes in section 8, which apply everywhere.

## What Weston asked for

**Golf first, order second.** Today, clicking a tee time treats it like an order: it jumps to
the register, loads charge lines into the cart, and the only golf controls are **+ modifier**
(which is for food and beverage) and **Guest Details**. The order should come *after* the golf.

Clicking a tee time should open the **reservation**. That's where you work the golf:
change the players and the player count, adjust tee fees, switch a player between 9 and 18
holes, open a customer profile, and see ID.me status. Only then does it go to the cart and
checkout. It should open as a **slide-over panel**, so you never lose your place on the tee
sheet. Weston said he could be convinced of a modal instead.

> "It's kind of pre — you're still massaging the reservation before you submit it to the
> cart and then check it out."

## Decisions

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Where the edits live | A separate **edition** of the same app (`src/pos/edition.tsx`), published as its own 18-hole prototypes. The base prototypes stay as they are. |
| How the reservation opens (tablet) | **Slide-over panel** from the right. The tee sheet (grid or list) **narrows** to the room left of it as it slides in: columns tighten, nothing is hidden under the panel, and there is no sideways scrolling |
| Where a customer profile opens | A **Customer tab** in the panel, for the selected player |
| ID.me | **Badge only**, from demo data. The verify flow waits on how Birdie shows it today |
| Bugs found in the recording | **Fixed everywhere**, not only in Weston Edits |
| What **Check in & pay** checks in | **Everyone** on the reservation, except no-shows — on the tablet and the phone |
| A player's fee after switching 9 ↔ 18 | The **rate card**: that hole count's price in the tee time's band (early / peak / twilight), on the player's rate class, or the row's price override. The booked length keeps the booking's own rate. Staff can override; Reset returns to the card |
| Rate class | **Per player**, not per booking: each seat follows its own customer — a **linked customer record**, or for the booker the **booking's phone**. **Never the seat's name**: a seat whose name looks like a customer shows a *Suggested profile · Link* in the Customer tab and pays the booking's rate until someone links it. Unlinked seats fall back to the booking's class. A member in a guest's group plays on the member rate, a guest in a member's group on rack. The same on the tablet, the phone and the register |
| Tax & cart fees per seat | **The seat's own class**, not the booking's status: each paying seat adds its own green-fee tax to the order's Taxes line and pays its own class's cart fee. A member's $0 seat carries no green-fee tax (no green fee, no tax on it), a guest in a member's booking pays the guest tax and cart fee. Paid and no-show seats add nothing |
| The phone's long-press quick sheet | **Open reservation** replaces its straight-to-register Check in & pay |
| Walk-ins | A **reservation too**: Walk-in (or a CHECK IN rate with nothing on the order) books the **next open tee time** after the demo "now", opens it in the panel / reservation screen, and staff can pick another time there. The base edition keeps its fast register-first walk-in |
| Tax | **One calculation, all editions** (`orderTotals`): golf by its booking's tax line, retail and F&B at the sales-tax rate, and the register, checkout, tip, reader and change due all charge that one total |
| Opening a reservation (tablet) | The tee sheet **scrolls it into view** (vertically) beside the panel, grid and list, without moving when it's already visible |
| Demo walk-ins | An `R-` code is a reservation: bookings the seed marked walk-in with one read `booked`. True walk-ins carry a `W-` code |
| Demo prices | **From the rate card**: every priced fixture is the card's price for its hole count, time band and class, so no nine costs more than the eighteen and switching a nine to 18 never lowers the fee. Members and comps stay $0 |
| Demo "now" | **One fixed clock** (`demoNow()`: May 21, 2026, 12:00 PM) for the now-line, the opening scroll, walk-ins, the rate card's default band and the times stamped on payments and activity, so every screenshot is the same whenever it's taken |

## Round 2: date navigation

A second Loom ([Request to Improve T Sheet Date Navigation](https://www.loom.com/share/a0d4ed98b7344746b5a4bf8a9d7ee4fb),
46 s). On the phone, tapping the date opened a fixed list of the 11 demo days, with no month
or year and no calendar. Getting to June took about a dozen taps on the next-day arrow. The
tablet already has a month calendar, so its controls stay as they are; the new navigation is
on the **phone**. Both get the same generated demo days, and all of it is **Weston Edits only**.

| # | Component | Tablet | Mobile |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Date Navigation** | The same controls (‹ ›, the calendar popover, **Today**, **Weekend**), now with a tee sheet on every date within a year of today: the same generated days as the phone, shared by the grid, list and day summary. The calendar dots those days and greys out dates beyond the year. A generated day that's been cleared stays empty | A swipeable **week strip** under the date for quick day hops, and the date row's ‹ › now move a week. Tapping the date opens a **calendar sheet** with a month grid, month and year jump, ‹ › by month, **Today**, dots on days with tee times, and the viewed date highlighted. Any date within a year either side of today has a believable, deterministic demo sheet; dates beyond that are greyed |

## How the edition works

An edition is a context, not a fork. Components read `useEdition()` and branch only where
Weston's edits differ, so one component serves both editions and nothing drifts into two
copies. A story picks one with `<Screen edition="weston" />` or `<MobileStory edition="weston" />`.
A prototype build picks one with `VITE_EDITION=weston`. In dev, add `?edition=weston` to the
prototype URL.

When Weston signs off, the edits become the default by changing one line in `edition.tsx`.
