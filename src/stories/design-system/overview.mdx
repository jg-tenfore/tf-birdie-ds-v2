<Section title="Principles" intro="Six rules every page applies. Each links to where it is explained in full.">
<Grid cols={3}>
  <NavCard eyebrow="1 · Density" title="Two densities, on purpose" to="foundations-spacing--overview" cta="Spacing">
    The counter terminal is dense and mouse-first: 12–14px text, 34px controls, 44px where a thumb is used. The handheld follows MD3 for a thumb: 16px body, 48dp targets. The same component is sized for each.
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="2 · One language" title="The tablet and the handheld look like one product" to="foundations-colors--overview" cta="Colors">
    Same tokens, same meanings: a PAID badge, a member dot, seat 2's blue and the kitchen's amber read the same on both. Only density and platform shapes differ.
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="3 · Money and state" title="Money and state are always on screen" to="foundations-typography--overview" cta="Typography">
    A payment button says its amount; a booking says whether it's paid; a dish says whether it has gone to the kitchen. One formatter writes every amount, so the row, the panel and the reader agree.
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="4 · Colour plus text" title="Colour never carries meaning alone" to="foundations-colors--overview" cta="Colors">
    Every status colour comes with its word (PAID, NO SHOW, Not sent) or an icon, and every icon-only control has a label for assistive tech.
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="5 · Light mode" title="Light, for a lit counter and the outdoors" to="foundations-colors--overview" cta="Why light only">
    The terminal sits in a bright pro shop and the handheld goes onto the course. The roles are semantic, so a dark scheme later is new tones, not new components. Sign-in is the one dark screen.
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="6 · Sample data" title="Deterministic, so every screenshot matches" to="start-here-style-guide--overview" cta="Style guide">
    The Dunes of Delgado, always Thursday, May 21, 2026 at noon. Bookings are generated by hashing ids, never the clock or a random number, so a screen looks the same whenever it's opened.
  </NavCard>
</Grid>
</Section>

<Section title="From token to screen" intro="Values flow one way. A colour or a size is decided once in tokens.ts, mapped by a theme, and read by components; screens never hold a value of their own.">

<Callout title="The rule.">
