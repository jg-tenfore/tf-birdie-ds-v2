<Section title="What to look at on each screen">
<Grid cols={3}>
  <NavCard eyebrow="Flow" title="Is the next step obvious?">
    At a busy counter, with a foursome waiting: can you get from the tee time to paid without hunting?
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="Money" title="Is it right, and does it say why?">
    Every price should name its rate, every discount its reason, and the total should match on every screen it appears.
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="Device" title="Does it hold on the hardware?">
    Try the Clover Mini, the large display and the Quest. Anything cramped, cut off or stretched too far?
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="Touch" title="Can you hit it?">
    The counter is mouse and touch; the handheld is a thumb. Any target too small or too close to another?
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="Words" title="Are these the words staff use?">
    Labels, buttons and messages. Weston has caught several already ("seat" is for food and beverage; "Link" meant nothing).
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="Buck" title="Who owns this setting?" to="start-here-buck-and-birdie--overview" cta="Buck and Birdie">
    If it's set up in Buck, the counter should read it, not ask for it again.
  </NavCard>
