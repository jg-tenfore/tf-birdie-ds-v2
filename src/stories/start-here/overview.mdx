<P>
  <strong>The tablet</strong> is the counter terminal: dense, mouse-and-touch, built for a pro shop counter, with every destination in v1's main nav (register, tee sheet, courts and bays, the restaurant, customers, refunds, gift cards, events, stock, the drawer and settings). <strong>The phone</strong> is the handheld staff carry: the same POS as a native Material Design 3 app, at touch size, with the tee sheet, the register, people and payment. Both run on the same state, data and pricing, so an order totals the same on either.
</P>
<P>
  <strong>Current is Weston Edits.</strong> The design the team works from is Weston's edits, made over five rounds in September: golf first, order second. On the tablet that is the <em>V1 → V2</em> edition, which is Weston's edits plus every other nav destination; on the phone it is the Weston Edits edition. Everything older, including the original port of the HTML prototype, is in <strong>Archive</strong>. The sample club is The Dunes of Delgado (the same name Buck uses), and "today" is always Thursday, May 21, 2026, at noon.
</P>

<Section title="Using this Storybook">
<Grid cols={3}>
  <NavCard eyebrow="Toolbar" title="Device" to="start-here-devices--overview" cta="Devices">
    Renders any story on a device: the frame takes that device's size. <strong>Auto</strong> keeps each story on its design canvas. A device only resizes stories of its own form.
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="Toolbar" title="Weston's four switches" to="reviews-sep-22-weston-edits-rounds-1-5-summary--overview" cta="Why they exist">
    <strong>Panel width</strong> (640, 820, cover), <strong>Row density</strong>, <strong>Transport</strong> (toggle or named rate) and <strong>Rate catalog</strong> (standard or 26 rates). They feed every story; a story that pins one keeps it.
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="Links" title="Every story has a link" to="start-here-deep-links--overview" cta="The deep-link scheme">
    Copy a story's URL from the address bar to point someone at an exact screen and state. The prototypes have their own deep links too.
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="For engineering" title="A screen is plain state" href="https://github.com/jg-tenfore/tf-birdie-ds-v2/blob/main/CLAUDE.md" cta="CLAUDE.md">
    One reducer drives the app, so a story describes the state it wants, <Code>&lt;Screen initialState=…/&gt;</Code>, and gets that screen. The prototypes and stories import the same components from <Code>src/pos</Code>.
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="For engineering" title="Tokens and theme" to="foundations-colors--overview" cta="Colors">
    <Code>src/theme/tokens.ts</Code> holds the MD3 roles, ported from the original's <Code>:root</Code>; <Code>theme.ts</Code> maps them onto MUI; <Code>mobile-theme.ts</Code> is the same tokens at touch density.
  </NavCard>
  <NavCard eyebrow="Archive" title="Something older?" to="archive-about-the-archive--overview" cta="About the archive">
    Every story that moved on Nov 26 kept its address, so a link shared before then still opens the same screen.
